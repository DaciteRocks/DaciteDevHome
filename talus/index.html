Talus

A desktop pet for Windows: a spider, a manta and a trilobite that live along the bottom of your screen, on top of whatever you are doing, and never get in the way of your mouse.

Recorded live on a Windows desktop. Every so often two creatures meet.

Before you run it

Talus is free and not code-signed, so Windows is cautious about it the first time.

SmartScreen (Windows 10 and 11). If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Expect this again for each new version you download.

Windows 11 only: Smart App Control. If a message names “Smart App Control”, it blocks unsigned apps every time they start, with no “Run anyway” button and no exception for one app. Talus can only be installed and run while it is turned off, under Windows Security > App & browser control > Smart App Control settings. An up-to-date Windows 11 lets you switch it back on later, but Talus will then be blocked again. If you would rather keep Smart App Control on, skip Talus for now. Windows 10 has no Smart App Control.

The SHA-256 of the installer is listed next to the download button, if you want to check your copy.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • WebView2 (already on Windows 11; the installer fetches it on Windows 10 if missing — be online).

How it works

  • Install. Run the setup file. It installs for your user only (no administrator password) into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Talus, adds a Start Menu entry, and offers a desktop shortcut.
  • Three creatures. A spider, a manta and a trilobite walk, swim and crawl along the bottom of one monitor. They are see-through to your mouse: clicks go straight to the window underneath. Now and then two of them meet. They pause while a full-screen app or game is in front.
  • Tray icon. Talus lives in the notification area by the clock (maybe under the ^ arrow). Right-click it to hide or pause the creatures, open the settings, or quit.
  • Settings window. It opens by itself the first time, and again from the tray’s Settings… or by launching Talus while it is already running. Choose the monitor, switch each creature on or off, set how often they meet, let the manta follow your cursor, and start Talus with Windows.
  • Uninstall. Quit from the tray icon, then Settings > Apps > Installed apps (Apps & features on Windows 10) > Talus > Uninstall. Tick “Delete the application data” to remove your settings too.

Changelog

v1.0.0 — 2026-09-30

  • First public release of Talus for Windows 10 and 11.
  • Three desktop creatures live along the bottom of your screen: a spider, a manta ray and a trilobite.
  • The trilobite crawls on eighteen legs that ripple in a wave down each side.
  • The spider and the trilobite never walk into each other: they stop, look, and the spider usually hops over.
  • The manta sometimes picks up the spider and carries it across the screen.
  • A Settings window (from the tray icon) picks the monitor, which creatures appear, how often they meet, and whether Talus starts with Windows.
  • Installs per user with no administrator prompt; uninstall from Windows Settings.
